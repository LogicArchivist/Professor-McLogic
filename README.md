You are Professor McLogic, the finest logic-type professor ever. In this game you will travel to many faraway lands and converse with the inhabitants. But beware; for many liars will cross your path, and it's up to you to determine who you can trust!!!

There are 10 scenarios in the game, each set in a different location with its own unique rules. Before playing a new scenario for the first time, read its info pages to understand how the characters behave and what you need to determine.

In each area, read every character's statements and use logic to deduce their type. When you're confident, submit your answer. You'll advance through 10 areas per difficulty run.

Three difficulty levels are available. Each run has 10 areas, and the puzzle sizes increase as you progress:

Classic: 3 × Two characters, 4 × Three characters, 3 × Four characters

Advanced: 3 × Three characters, 4 × Four characters, 3 × Five characters

Expert: 3 × Four characters, 4 × Five characters, 3 × Six characters

Best times are saved per scenario and difficulty level.

T/F Marking: Click the dot (·) before any statement to mark it as True (T) or False (F). Useful for tracking your deductions as you work through a puzzle.

Elimination Helper: Click "Show elimination helper" to reveal type buttons for each character. Clicking a button cycles it through: neutral → soft confirm → soft eliminate → hard confirm → hard eliminate.

Checkpoint: Click "📌 Save checkpoint" to save your current elimination and T/F mark state. If a branch leads nowhere, click "↩ Restore" to return to that saved point.

Practice mode lets you solve individual puzzles of any character count (2–6) without affecting your timed records. It's a great way to get familiar with a new scenario's mechanics.

Best times are recorded per scenario and difficulty. You can clear records from the scenario menu if you want a fresh start.

Windwhip Peak only: Use the "Converse →" button on each character card to view the converse form of their statements, which can help when solving Dark dragon puzzles.
